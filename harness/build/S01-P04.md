## Limitations / remaining Season1 risk

- All performance is DESKTOP PROXY ONLY; no physical Android/iOS, sustained thermal/memory/touch or device multitouch proof. This phase did not add SDK/templates/signing or change export setup. Linux cannot prove iOS packaging; P00 artifact availability is not a shipped mobile build.

Draw-call/instancing granularity, native generation/streaming and device budgets require extended mobile profiling. Low-poly opaque art intentionally avoids alpha overdraw; textured cutout/mipmap/coverage validation awaits actual foliage art.
- Mob navigation is lightweight direct steering, not a final obstacle-aware navigation/raid ecosystem. Regional movement approximates macro-height; distant authoritative deadlines are evaluated centrally. Very large populations/connected support networks require measured scheduler/recompute budgets. Passive corpse expiry intentionally loses unclaimed delicate loot; protected bags persist. Full player death/respawn/penalties, complete craft/workbench UI/catalogs/weather/social/caves remain deferred.
