# S01-P03 — Player Foundation

## Objective

Replace the P02 inspection pawn with the real player on the generated island: 3D movement, two camera modes, composed LPC presentation, targeting, inventory state and normal/heavy/dodge combat proof.

## Scope

- In: modular/tunable `CharacterBody3D` PlayerMotor with feet contact, camera-relative walk/run, acceleration, gravity, slope/floor snapping and stamina seam.
- In: elevated angled gameplay camera with obstruction baseline; reversible third-person Build camera with accurate terrain/BuildGrid aiming; no baseline first person.
- In: narrow final-schema LPC compiler/catalog/composition/cache slice, directional Idle/Move plus an action, safe missing-clip fallback and semantic action resolver.
- In: central InteractionTargeter; real inventory/equipment/hotbar state and minimal usable UI; data-driven attack/dodge/stat interfaces and authoritative save integration.
- Out: full weapon/skills/Ultimate/guard/parry library, complete character creator, props/farming, chest/workbench scope, mobs/NPCs and caves.
- Expected files/subsystems: player/input/camera/combat/inventory scripts/resources/scenes, LPC data/generated/presentation, save adapter, SeasonTest and focused regressions.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The real player traverses tested smooth slopes/steps without obvious tunneling/floating; hot paths avoid allocation-heavy work. Gameplay and Build cameras transition both ways without losing readable terrain/player targeting.
2. LPC stable catalog IDs drive composed/cached Sprite3D visuals; layers are not permanent per-layer Sprite3Ds. Directional Idle/Move and an action work; missing clips fall back and equal appearances reuse cache resources where implemented.
3. Gameplay requests semantic actions (`IDLE`, `MOVE`, `RUN`, `MELEE_SLASH`, `HURT`); raw upstream filenames/animation names remain behind the resolver.
4. One authoritative targeter distinguishes interactable, terrain/voxel, BuildGrid cell/socket and combat targets, with configurable priorities.
5. Inventory has 20 slots (5x4 UI), eight hotbar slots and Helmet/Chest/Legs/Accessory 1/Accessory 2 equipment; stable definitions differ from stack instances, constraints pass and no weight system is added.
6. Normal attack works; held Attack enters HeavyAim without executing, direction aims per weapon rule and release executes exactly once. Dodge has deterministic i-frames; one attack does not repeatedly hit the same target unless explicitly multihit; no default contact damage.
7. Damage/stat seam follows `raw_damage * 100 / (100 + Defense)` without building the full progression system. Attack/moveset definitions stay data-driven.
8. Save/reload preserves implemented transform/spawn, inventory/equipment/hotbar and appearance IDs; composed textures and transient attack/camera state are not persisted.
9. P02 streaming still works during player boundary crossings; boot/save/world regressions pass. Record tests, controls, LPC schema slice and platform-labelled profiling in `harness/build/S01-P03.md`, review diff/secrets/local paths, commit/push and hand off before P04.

- Touch aiming, camera obstruction and real-device performance require separate proof; desktop tests remain proxies.
