## C01 — Water Foundation

### Current audited gap
- `GeneratedWaterView` builds flat lake/river meshes.
- `OceanMaterial` only colors the surface; it does not displace geometry.
- `WorldWater.sample()` reports depth but `current` is always `Vector3.ZERO`.
- No dynamic flow solver, buoyancy, swimming, waterfall connection, watering-can refill, rain contribution or terrain-triggered redistribution.

### Required architecture
Implement water as a lightweight 2D heightfield/grid over the editable 3D voxel terrain, not as water voxels and not as per-cell Nodes.

Suggested data per active water cell/tile:
- terrain height
- water depth / surface height
- flow X/Z
- volume
- activity/sleep flags

Rules:
- tiled/chunked simulation near relevant active regions
- simulation at a lower fixed rate than rendering (target ~10–20 Hz, benchmark and tune)
- visual interpolation between solver steps
- sleeping settled water; wake on neighboring flow, terrain edit, source/rain change or other relevant invalidation
- static/cheap ocean reservoir; do not simulate the entire ocean
- active simulation for inland water/coast interaction zones
- terrain edits can dam/release water
- ordinary structures do not participate in the solver in R3; future dedicated water-barrier component may
- waterfall connections are derived when flow crosses a sufficiently large vertical delta; waterfall rendering can be a ribbon/mesh + shader/foam/splash/audio while the authority remains the heightfield

### Water rendering profiles
High / Medium / Low-Compatibility must preserve the same water semantics while changing cost.

High may use moving normals, depth coloration, flow distortion, shoreline foam, interaction ripples and richer refraction/reflection where supported.
Medium uses cheaper normals/depth/flow/foam.
Low/Compatibility keeps depth color + cheap normal/flow animation and simplified foam without expensive screen-space features.

### Gameplay contracts
- player/mob submerged query
- buoyancy/current force seam
- swim state seam
- watering can has finite capacity/current volume and can refill from valid freshwater
- WaterGrid receives terrain invalidation from voxel edits
- weather can add water later through an explicit API

### C01 acceptance
1. Lakes/rivers are not merely flat colored triangles from a gameplay-domain perspective.
2. A bounded active water tile can redistribute water downhill and settle/sleep.
3. Terrain edit can change local water routing after invalidation.
4. `WorldWater.sample()` can return non-zero current in moving water.
5. Settled water sleeps and does not continuously solve at render frequency.
6. Ocean remains cheap and does not allocate a solver across the entire sea.
7. Player has a usable submerged/current/swim seam.
8. Watering can volume/refill contract exists and is validated against inland water.
9. High/Medium/Low-Compatibility renderer paths preserve semantics.
10. Existing R0/R1/R2 saves migrate without reset.
11. Godot 4.7.2 smoke/regression passes; no project errors/warnings.
12. Performance evidence records solver active/sleeping cost and renderer cost separately.

## C02 — Inventory + Crafting Foundation

### Inventory interaction
Replace the temporary select + SpinBox transfer UI with production semantics:
- tap: item detail popup
- double tap: full-stack transfer to paired inventory
- long press + drag: drag/drop
- same inventory: move / merge / swap without transfer modal
- cross-inventory drag: amount/split modal
- slot targeting and automatic first-free-slot behavior where appropriate
- merge/swap must remain transactional and revision-safe

### Split modal
Horizontal quantity slider with clear left/right destination semantics, Cancel and Confirm.
Quick split actions in item popup:
- split 1
- split half
- split all (one unit into each available empty slot until slots or quantity are exhausted)

### Spoilage
Introduce a modular spoilage policy and persist freshness in stack instance data or a typed equivalent.
Required semantics:
- one unit spoils when timer reaches zero; timer resets for next unit in that stack
- split copies/inherits source freshness
- merging adopts the destination stack's freshness for transferred units
- splitting into multiple stacks means each stack subsequently ticks independently
- spoiled result item is data-driven

### Crafting
Implement the Foundation crafting domain currently absent:
- recipe definitions
- craft job
- 5-slot craft queue
- cancel/reorder contiguous jobs
- timer is not authority
- on completion, atomically revalidate/consume resources then create output
- output inserts into destination; overflow becomes world drop without stopping queue
- workbench/station resource scope can include player + authorized nearby containers
- stale revisions cause replan/reject; no duplication
